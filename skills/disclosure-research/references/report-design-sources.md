# Report design sources

The template is an original adaptation of common research-note structure, not a bank's proprietary report. CFA Institute's public [Equity Research Report Essentials](https://www.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/-/media/documents/support/research-challenge/challenge/rc-equity-research-report-essentials.pdf) describes basic information, business/industry context, an investment summary, financial analysis, valuation, and risks. Checked 2026-10-03.

For this product, the evidence ledger and publication timing come before optional company valuation. A disclosed purchase alone does not establish intrinsic value. Catalysts, competing explanations, and falsification conditions make the reasoning reviewable. Use financial analysis and valuation only if the required sourced inputs exist; this bundle supplies no stock-rating system.

Public institutional research can also illustrate conditional scenario framing. Goldman Sachs' [AI innovation and inflation portfolio discussion](https://www.goldmansachs.com/insights/articles/balancing-the-risks-to-portfolios-from-an-innovation-boom-and-inflation) connects possible investment upside to overcapacity and inflation risks. This skill uses its own scenario format and makes no claim of Goldman Sachs endorsement. Checked 2026-10-03.
